C · Synopsis

What is DPMS?

DPMS (Design Project Management System) is a digital ecosystem developed by the coordinator team to manage and assess the NMK31704 Design Project course, from forming groups to each student's final mark.

Why it was built182 students, 3 programmes, 43 groups and 76 evaluators could no longer be managed with Word files, email and paper.
What changedEvery stage of the course now happens in one digital flow, with weekly evidence for every group.
What it made possibleAuthentic, multi-source assessment aligned to 7 course learning outcomes, which is complex and time-consuming to run by hand for 182 students.

AIPDP UniMAP 2026 · Alternative Assessment11
D. Rationale10%

Objectives of the innovation

  1. Develop DPMS, a digital ecosystem that runs the course from forming groups to each student's final mark, replacing the manual process.
  2. Assess the process, not just the product. Weekly logbook and Gantt progress, verified by the supervisor with feedback before summative assessment.
  3. Make tasks authentic and aligned. Projects solve real problems, and every component is mapped to the 7 COs.
  4. Recognise individual contribution. 20% of the marks are individual: supervisor observation, 7-criteria peer review and presentation quality.
  5. Improve validity and reliability. Three evaluators, the same e-rubric with descriptors, automatic marking-gap checks.

E1 · Assessment design

Rubrics with descriptors: every mark has a clear meaning

Every course rubric (report, presentation, supervisor observation, logbook, peer review) uses the same 0–5 scale, and every level has a written descriptor. Different evaluators read a mark the same way, and students know from the start what is expected.

Example: criterion D4 SDG considerationreport rubric · CO2 (PO6)

AIPDP UniMAP 2026 · Alternative Assessment32
E3 · After evaluation

Triangulation and the marking-gap check

Assessment method

Marks from several evaluators and sources are combined (triangulation), and large differences between evaluators are reviewed before marks are finalised.

How DPMS helps

The 15% marking-gap check flags groups automatically; final marks, grades and CO-PO analysis are generated with no manual calculation.

What the data shows

Validity: every CO is assessed through more than one piece of evidence (except CO5 & CO7), and every criterion is mapped to the CO it assesses.

Consistency: Panel 1 and Panel 2 averages are almost the same: report 7.89 versus 8.03, presentation 7.80 versus 7.92 (out of 10). Because marks cluster in a narrow range, the system flags every discrepancy for the coordinator to review.

Accuracy: the automatically calculated marks can be reproduced exactly for 182/182 students when checked against the official CO-PO record.

Interpretation

Components whose method changed (weekly logbook, Gantt, supervisor observation and peer review) rose by +6.2 to +10.6 points. Components only moved to an e-rubric rose by +2.6 to +5.7.

These are two different cohorts, so we do not claim this method is the only cause. But the larger rises in CO6 and CO4 are consistent with the assessment changes made.

Dissemination & sustainability

Can be adopted by any project course. The assessment model (process, peer, product; three evaluators; e-rubrics with descriptors) can be used by other project courses. The system runs on UniMAP's Google Workspace with no licence cost; rubric criteria, weights, weeks and roles are stored as data.
