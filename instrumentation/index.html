TRACE · C. Synopsis7
C The name

What TRACE stands for

In measurement, traceability links every reading to a trusted standard. TRACE links every student's practice to a course outcome.

T

Try

Every exercise asks for the student's own answer first. No "show answer" button exists.

R

Reveal

Each wrong try reveals a hint. The full working is revealed only after a correct answer.

A

Apply

Live calculators, circuit diagrams and virtual lab benches apply each idea to real circuits.

C

Calibrate

The game, the Pre-Lab Check and the class board let students check and adjust their own level.

E

Excel

XP, levels and ranks reward steady effort, and lecturers see who needs help before the exam.

TRACE · C. The name8
D · 10% Rasional · What motivated the change?

Students watched, but did not practise

Instrumentation is a calculation-heavy, hands-on course. Before TRACE, four problems kept coming back.

🪑Passive lecturesSlides and worked examples were watched, not tried. Mistakes surfaced only in the test.
📄No practice between classesStatic notes gave no feedback, so students did not know whether their answer was right.
🔌Unprepared for the labStudents met the ESP32, the wiring and the code for the first time at the bench, with three hours to finish.
👀Struggles stayed hiddenWith 61 students in three lab sections, the lecturer could not see who was falling behind until the marks came in.

E · 40% Pendekatan · What method is used?

Students try first; the working comes after

Every one of the 82 exercises follows the same loop. There is no "show answer" button anywhere on the site.

Step 1AnswerType your own value and unit.
Step 2Hint 1A wrong try unlocks a guiding hint.
Step 3Hint 2Another try unlocks a second hint. Never the number.
Step 4Working unlockedAll correct? The full working opens.
82%of 1,953 solved exercises were right on the first try
18%solved after a hint: students kept trying

TRACE · E. Approach12
E · 40% Interactive chapters

Live circuits that answer back

Change a resistor and the diagram, the step-by-step working and the oscilloscope update at once. Push the gain too far and the output clips at the supply rail, just like a real op-amp.

  • Formula → numbers → result for every step, so students see how, not just what.
  • Warnings such as saturation or "the shortcut does not apply" appear when they matter.
  • Phone-first: every chapter works at 360 px wide, in light and dark mode.
▶ Change the resistors yourself

TRACE · E. Approach16
E · 40% AI-assisted learning

An AI helper that guides, but never gives the answer

"Ask Dr. Zamri" is on every page. It explains ideas, but for exercises, quizzes and lab tasks it only gives hints.

StudentWhat is the output voltage for Exercise 2? Just tell me the number.
Ask Dr. Zamri · AILet's work it out together. For an inverting amplifier, what is the gain formula? Once you have the gain, multiply it by Vin. What do you get?
StudentGain = −200/48 = −4.17, so about −29 V?
Ask Dr. Zamri · AIGood working. Now type it into the exercise and press Check. Also think: can a ±15 V op-amp really output −29 V?

Illustration of a typical exchange.

🛡️Answer guardReplies are checked against every unsolved exercise; an answer is replaced with "work it out, I'll guide you".
💤Rests in battleDuring an INSTRUMENTA battle the helper rests. Only Volt's hints are allowed.
🤖HonestIt says it is an AI when asked, and never shares private details.

TRACE was designed by the team. Generative AI (Claude, by Anthropic) was used as a coding and writing assistant to build parts of the website and this e-book, under the team leader's direction. All course content, exercises, answers and figures were checked by the team. The "Ask Dr. Zamri" helper on the site uses a large language model, is labelled as an AI, and is limited to hints.
